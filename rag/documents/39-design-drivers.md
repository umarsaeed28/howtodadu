# Architectural and Design Drivers

> Team notes from a Seattle builder's talk. Market and design guidance, not code.

Also called: backyard cottage, DADU, detached ADU.

## Functional layout
Include a dedicated home office or guest room. Couples who both work from home need it.

## Identity and street frontage
- Two stories are preferred over three.
- Covered, convenient parking adds value.
- Clear street frontage makes the unit feel like an independent single-family home, "my house", rather than a unit behind someone else's house.

## How this connects to layout
Side-by-side layouts give each unit its own street frontage, which matches what buyers want. Rear units with no frontage and no parking are the hardest to sell.
