# Accessory dwelling unit rules (SMC 23.42.022)

> Team summary of the current Seattle ADU rules in SMC 23.42.022, which replaced the old DADU section SMC 23.44.041. Confirm a specific number against the code text before relying on it for a permit.

Also called: ADU, DADU, AADU, detached accessory dwelling unit, attached accessory dwelling unit, backyard cottage, stacked ADU.

## How many ADUs per lot
How many ADUs can I build? Up to two ADUs on a lot that has a principal dwelling unit (the main house). They can be any combination: attached (AADU), detached (DADU), or stacked.

## Size limits
How big can an ADU be? An ADU with 2 bedrooms or fewer is capped at 1,000 square feet of gross floor area. An ADU with 3 or more bedrooms is capped at 1,200 square feet of gross floor area.

## Floor area that does not count
These areas are excluded from an ADU's gross floor area:
- Stories that are fully underground.
- Up to 250 square feet of an attached garage.
- Up to 35 square feet of long-term bike storage.

## Parking
Does an ADU need parking? No. No off-street parking is required for any ADU anywhere in Seattle.

## Selling an ADU as a condominium
Can an ADU be sold separately? Yes. An individual ADU may be sold as a condominium unit.

## Height
How tall can an ADU be? The height limit matches the zone or the principal structure, up to 32 feet in most Neighborhood Residential (NR) zones.

## Rear setback
What is the rear setback for a DADU? 5 feet. If the lot abuts an alley, no rear setback is required.
