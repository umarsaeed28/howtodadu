# Lot Size and Dimensions

> Team guide for Seattle DADU screening. Supplied by the project team, not copied from the Seattle Municipal Code. Treat numbers here as screening rules and confirm them against the current SMC before design.

Also called: backyard cottage, DADU, detached accessory dwelling unit, detached ADU.

## Minimum lot area
A lot needs at least 3,200 sq ft of total area to build a DADU in Seattle. Below that, the lot fails the area check.

## Standard lot width
- A 40 ft wide lot, such as 40 ft by 100 ft (4,000 sq ft), is very common in Seattle. It usually suits a single DADU.
- A lot 45 ft to 55 ft wide is much more flexible. It can take a side driveway or units placed side by side.

## Deep lots in North Seattle
Lots 40 to 50 ft wide and 123 to 125 ft deep are common in Northgate, Haller Lake and nearby. They generally cannot fit side-by-side units. They need a stacked or staggered front-to-back layout.

## Numbers this guide does not give
The guide does not give exact side-yard setbacks or a maximum lot coverage percentage. When a question needs one of those, say that the knowledge base does not specify the exact numerical limit, and that lot width and where the existing house sits decide whether a driveway and setbacks work.
