# Height and Bulk

> Team guide for Seattle DADU screening. The height figure below is from the team guide and is not verified against the current Seattle Municipal Code. Mark it unverified when you rely on it.

Also called: backyard cottage, DADU, detached accessory dwelling unit, detached ADU.

## Height
How tall can a backyard cottage be? A DADU can be built up to 3 stories (floors) tall, up from the earlier 2-story limit.

## The trade-off
Three stories let you fit more floor area or more units on a small lot. Over-building hurts livability, privacy and outdoor space. Balance height against yard space and light.
