# Land Strategy and Execution

> Team notes from a Seattle builder's talk. Strategy, not code.

Also called: lot acquisition, DADU development, infill.

## Target locations
- Larger lots, for example 8,000 sq ft lots in North Seattle and Broadview, give the most room for more units.
- City-adjacent markets such as Kirkland and Burien are also targets.

## First-mover opportunity
Many sellers have not yet priced in the expanded 6 to 10 unit capacity. Land bought before prices adjust can be underpriced relative to what it can hold.

## Validate feasibility before buying
Work with an architect and a builder who builds in Seattle to check real construction costs against the theoretical code density before you buy. The density a lot allows is not the density that pencils out.
